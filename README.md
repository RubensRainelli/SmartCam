## ✨ What SmartCam Can Do for You

* **🎨 Blur or Replace Your Background (No Green Screen Needed):** Hide a messy room with natural studio portrait blur, replace your background with built-in office scenes or your own photos, or use a physical Green Screen with automatic one-click color calibration.
* **📱 Use Your Smartphone as an HD Wireless Webcam:** Don't have a good webcam on your computer or laptop? Scan the QR code shown inside SmartCam with your phone's camera (iPhone or Android) to stream live HD video over your home Wi-Fi — **zero apps to install on your phone**.
* **💻 Built for Windows, macOS & Linux:** Enjoy the same fast, native studio experience whether you use a Windows PC, an Apple Silicon or Intel Mac, or your favorite Linux desktop distribution.
* **🎥 Works Everywhere Out of the Box:** Appears as the **"SmartCam"** camera inside **Zoom, Microsoft Teams, Google Meet, Discord, Skype, OBS Studio**, and all modern browsers (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Brave, Safari).
* **🔒 100% Private, Offline & Ultra-Lightweight:** Every video frame is processed directly on your computer. No video ever leaves your device, no account is required, and there are no cloud subscriptions or watermarks.

## 🚀 Quick Start in 3 Simple Steps

1. **Launch SmartCam on Your Computer:**
   * **Windows:** Run `SmartCam-Setup-v2.0.0-x64.exe` and open **SmartCam** from the Start Menu.
   * **macOS:** Open `SmartCam-v2.0.0-macos-universal.dmg`, drag **SmartCam** to Applications, and launch it.
   * **Linux:** Run `SmartCam-v2.0.0-x86_64.AppImage` (or launch `smartcam` after installing the `.deb` package).
2. **Choose Your Look or Connect Your Phone:** Pick your preferred background effect (**Blur**, **Studio Image**, or **Solid Color**), adjust the framing (`16:9`, `4:3`, `1:1`, or `9:16`), or click **Smartphone Wi-Fi** to scan the QR code with your phone.
3. **Select "SmartCam" in Your Video Call App:** Open **Zoom, Microsoft Teams, Google Meet, Discord, or OBS Studio**, go to your Video Settings, and select **SmartCam** as your camera.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) & Compatibility

### Which operating systems and video apps are supported in SmartCam v2.0?
* **🐧 Linux:** Supports any 64-bit Linux distribution via the universal **AppImage**, Debian/Ubuntu `.deb` package, or portable `.tar.gz` archive (integrating automatically with standard V4L2 webcams and `v4l2loopback` virtual cameras).
* **🍎 macOS:** Native Universal binary supporting both **Apple Silicon (M1 / M2 / M3 / M4)** and **Intel** Macs running macOS 11 Big Sur or newer.
* **🪟 Windows:** Built for modern **Windows 10 and Windows 11 (64-bit)** using the native Windows Media Foundation camera platform for instant compatibility with Teams, Zoom, Meet, Discord, OBS, and browsers.
  > *Using an older legacy Windows program that requires DirectShow?* Support for legacy DirectShow-only programs was retired in v2.0 to keep SmartCam lightweight and modern. You can still download **[SmartCam v1.x from the Releases page](https://github.com/RubensRainelli/SmartCam/releases)**.

### How can I verify the integrity of my download?
Every official GitHub Release includes a [`SHA256SUMS.txt`](https://github.com/RubensRainelli/SmartCam/releases/download/v2.0.0/SHA256SUMS.txt) file containing the cryptographic SHA-256 checksums of all packages:
* **Linux / macOS:** `sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS.txt --ignore-missing` (or `shasum -a 256 -c SHA256SUMS.txt`)
* **Windows (PowerShell):** `Get-FileHash .\SmartCam-Setup-v2.0.0-x64.exe -Algorithm SHA256`
